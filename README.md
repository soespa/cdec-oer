# CDEC – Open Educational Resources

Open educational material of the **Climate Data Entrepreneurial Club (CDEC)**, published at **[material.cdec.io](https://material.cdec.io)**.

CDEC introduces students in grades 10–13 to data analysis, artificial intelligence and satellite data. Learners work with real environmental datasets and develop their own project ideas at the intersection of environmental informatics and sustainability.

This repository contains the Jupyter-based material and environment that accompany the website. The material is German-language.

## Modules

1. **Fundamentals of remote sensing**, including an introduction to Jupyter Notebooks
2. **Data literacy and AI education in Python**, using real datasets such as urban air quality
3. **Measurable climate change**
4. **Entrepreneurial education**, with a focus on creative teamwork
5. **Pitch Your Idea**, presenting to audiences such as investors or politicians

The website also offers a getting-started guide, data projects, reusable code snippets (for example, downloading satellite data) and a glossary.
